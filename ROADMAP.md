# Roadmap

Goal: the easiest way for online service providers (VAs, OBMs, social media managers, etc.)
to edit talking ("yapping") reels and turn one video into lots of content.
Everything should be swappable and quick to redo.

## Now: Version A, service providers making their own content
Get this right and tested on real reels before building Version B.

Done
- Upload from phone, transcription with word timings, export a 1080×1920 MP4
- Tabs: Cut, Visuals, Sound, Finish
- Cut by the word, remove pauses, cut ums
- Hook (position, font, colour, duration) and captions (phrase/one word, bubble/coloured/serif key words)
- Post caption writer + "About your business" profile
- Light theme with brand purple

Next (in rough order)
1. Put it online (Railway) and test on real reels
2. Trial reels: after exporting, create 3 versions of the same reel to post as Instagram trial reels
3. Hook library by role (VA, OBM, social media manager, …)
4. Saved call-to-action keywords ("Comment VA", "DM me AUDIT")
5. Content idea starters ("Not sure what to film?")
6. Reel to carousel (slides in your brand)
7. Client wins: testimonial to quote card, and client case study carousel
8. Sound: background music that ducks under the voice, sound effects
9. Weekly batch: upload several videos, get back a styled posting queue with captions

## Later: Version B, VAs editing reels for their clients (upgrade)
- A separate brand kit and business profile per client
- Switch between clients
- A share link the client can open to review and approve a reel
- Positioned as "offer reel editing as a paid service"
- Will need real accounts/logins and per-customer data before selling
